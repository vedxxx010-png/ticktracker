Study tips

  1. Test yourself instead of re-reading: flashcards, covered answers, explaining aloud.
  2. Solve every NCERT example and exercise, then previous year papers (at least the last 5).
  3. Draw and label key science diagrams; they carry marks.
  4. Use mind maps for Science and Social Science; keep short notes of formulas, definitions and dates.
  5. Practise timed writing for languages and SST: format, vocabulary, structure.
  6. Study 45–60 minutes, then break 5–10 minutes; take 30 minutes after every 2–3 hours.
  7. Sleep 7–8 hours, eat balanced meals, drink water, walk a little each day.
  8. Give extra time to weak areas, and stay calm: revision refines what you know.
Good luck with your exams.
